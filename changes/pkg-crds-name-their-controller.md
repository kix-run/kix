No configuration changes are needed. `victoria-metrics-operator`, `arc`, `cdi`, `cert-manager`, `clickhouse-operator`, `envoy-gateway-controller`, `external-secrets`, `infisical-operator`, `kubevirt`, `mayastor`, `prometheus-operator`, `rabbitmq-operator`, `snapshot-controller`, `stackgres-operator`, `tailscale`, `velero`, `volsync`, and `zfs-localpv` now declare a `controller` workload for the custom resource kinds they reconcile. On the first deploy after upgrading, Kix re-applies existing custom resources of these kinds to update their `kix.run/depends-on` annotation. When you prune custom resources of these kinds together with their controller, Kix now deletes the custom resources first and waits until they are gone before removing the controller. If a delete times out (`[K2794]`), Kix names the controller and reports whether it is ready.
