Remove single-dash spellings of reserved flags from `extraArgs` on `prometheus-operator`, `snapshot-controller`, `arc`, and `spegel` instances, and spellings with three or more leading dashes on `spegel`. These four programs treat `-flag` and `--flag` as the same flag, and `spegel` accepts any number of leading dashes. Previously, those packages checked only `--flag`, so alternate dash counts bypassed the check and could override settings the package manages. Setting any of them in `extraArgs` now stops the build with `[K8006]`. On `prometheus-operator`, remove `-web.listen-address`, `-kubelet-service`, and `-prometheus-config-reloader` (set `operandImages.prometheus-config-reloader` instead). On `snapshot-controller`, remove `-leader-election`, `-leader-election-namespace`, `-http-endpoint`, and `-metrics-path`. On `arc`, remove `-metrics-addr`, `-listener-metrics-addr`, `-listener-metrics-endpoint`, `-health-probe-bind-address`, `-enable-leader-election`, `-leader-election-id`, and `-auto-scaling-runner-set-only`. On `spegel`, remove any single-dash or three-or-more-dash form of `registry-addr`, `router-addr`, `metrics-addr`, `containerd-sock` (set `cluster.containerd.socket`), `containerd-content-path` (set `cluster.containerd.contentPath`), `containerd-namespace`, `containerd-registry-config-path` (set `cluster.containerd.registryConfigPath`), `bootstrap-kind`, `dns-bootstrap-domain`, `http-bootstrap-url`, `http-bootstrap-cert-dir`, `static-bootstrap-peers`, `mirrored-registries` (set `mirroredRegistries`), and `data-dir`.
