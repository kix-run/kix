In every built-in flavor, `platform-dns` now imports the cluster's DNS Service, so `out.name` and `out.fqdn` both name that Service. On `rke2`, `out.name` and `out.fqdn` now point to `rke2-coredns-rke2-coredns`, the DNS Service RKE2 installs, instead of `coredns` and `kube-dns`, which RKE2 does not create. Before, `platform-dns` imported the DNS Deployment, so `out.name` held the Deployment name while `out.fqdn` pointed to the Service. When `platform-dns` is active, `kix deploy` now waits until the DNS Service has a ready endpoint before deploying workloads that depend on DNS. Activating `platform-dns` on `gke-autopilot` or `openshift` still times out during `kix deploy`, as the old Deployment check did. Kix looks for an imported Service in the `platform-dns` instance's namespace, which is `gke-managed` on `gke-autopilot` (while `kube-dns` is in `kube-system`) and `kube-system` on `openshift` (while `dns-default` is in `openshift-dns`).
