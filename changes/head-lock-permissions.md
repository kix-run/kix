Kix now installs and updates its own four CustomResourceDefinitions (`activations.kix.run`, `packageinstances.kix.run`, `activationheads.kix.run`, and `packageinstancerevisions.kix.run`) before it changes a cluster. Before upgrading, grant every Kubernetes account that runs `kix deploy`, `kix rollback`, `kix gc`, or `kix migrate domain`, and the Kix operator's account, `get` on those four CRDs. The account that runs the first upgraded Kix on a cluster also needs `create` on `customresourcedefinitions` to install the CRDs that are missing, and `update` on them when a later Kix brings a newer definition. Grant every such account `get`, `list`, `create`, and `update` on `activationheads.kix.run`, which holds the deploy lock. If you use operator mode, upgrade the Kix operator before running `kix deploy --operator` or `kix rollback --operator`. Commands stop before changing anything when these permissions are missing, reporting `[K9557]` when Kix cannot read, create, or update one of its CRDs, and `--operator` commands stop with `[K9557]` until the upgraded operator lists `head-lock` in `kix.run/operator-features` on `activations.kix.run`.
