Pass `--include unattributed` to `kix gc --full-scan` to delete stale Kix-labeled objects that do not match any deployment record on the cluster. By default, `kix gc --full-scan` now lists these objects under "Objects with no matching deployment record" and keeps them, and the Kix operator leaves them alone, even when only one Kix cluster has deployment records on the Kubernetes cluster. With `--include unattributed`, `kix gc --full-scan` compares them against the active deployment's build (or the build from your local files when the cluster has no active deployment) and deletes any that are not in it. Leftover objects from a deploy or rollback without `--prune` do not need this flag: while a deployment record lists their build under `status.orphansKeptFrom`, `kix gc --full-scan` still links them to that Kix cluster and deletes them even after `kix gc` retires the record that deployed them. An object has no matching record when `kix gc` retired its deployment records and no remaining record lists its build under `status.orphansKeptFrom`, when it predates `kix.run/activations`, or when it belonged to another Kix cluster whose records were deleted. Previously, once another Kix cluster's deployment records were deleted, `kix gc --full-scan` or the Kix operator could mistake that cluster's remaining objects for its own and delete them.
