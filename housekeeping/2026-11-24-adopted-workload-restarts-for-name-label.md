# 2026-11-24 · `kix/lib/scope/builders.nix` · Adopting an existing workload restarts its pods when the pod template lacks `app.kubernetes.io/name`.

Adopting a live `Deployment` that has only `app: web` on its pods replaces every pod on the first `kix deploy`, even when all other fields match. `mkWorkload` keeps `spec.selector` unchanged, but always adds `app.kubernetes.io/name` (set to the instance name) to `spec.template.metadata.labels`. `kix.mkEnvVars` and `out.mkEnv` (`kix/lib/core/resource.nix`) also sort container `env` entries by name, which triggers a rollout when the live workload listed variables in another order. Neither `kix plan` nor the adoption guides (`take-ownership-of-kubectl-managed-resources.mdx`, `preserve-existing-selectors.mdx`) warn about these restarts.
