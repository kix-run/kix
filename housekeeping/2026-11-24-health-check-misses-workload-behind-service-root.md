# 2026-11-24 · `kix/lib/vocab/health-check.nix` · `kix.healthCheck` does not rerun its probe Job on workload changes when `self.root` is a Service and `requires` omits the workload.

`kix.healthCheck` reruns its probe Job when `self.root` or a resource in `requires` changes, using a `checksum/root` annotation that holds the content hash of the root's own manifest. A Service manifest does not change when its workload's image, config, or pod spec changes. Packages such as `clickhouse`, `clickhouse-cluster`, `loki`, and `plausible` make a Service their root without listing the workload in `requires`, so version bumps and config changes deploy without rerunning the probe. During kind testing of the `nats` package on 2026-11-24, three deploys that rolled the StatefulSet kept the `nats-health` Job from the first deploy. The `nats` package now lists its StatefulSet in `requires`. The `kix.healthCheck` section of `docs/src/content/docs/reference/composition-helpers.mdx` documents the rerun rules, and `housekeeping/2026-11-13-rerun-jobs-list-checksums-by-hand.md` records the related rerun-Job checksum problem.
