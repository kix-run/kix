# 2026-11-18 · `kix/packages/stable/victoria-metrics-{agent,alert}` · A version upgrade finishes its deploy and runs its health checks while the old vmagent and vmalert pods still serve.

Seen when moving VictoriaMetrics from 1.150.0 to 1.156.0 on kind with `test-victoria-metrics`. `kix deploy` printed "Deploy complete" with the VMAgent and VMAlert CRs `operational` and the `vm-agent-health` and `vm-alert-health` Jobs completed, while the operator was still replacing the old pods: the old vmagent and vmalert pods were Terminating and their new pods were 29 seconds old. The health checks can pass against the old version, so a new version that fails to start is not caught by the deploy. The readiness the package declares on the CR (`/status/updateStatus`) does not wait for the operator's Deployment rollout. Look at how the VM operator sets `updateStatus` and whether the CRs' readiness should also check `observedGeneration` or the Deployment.
