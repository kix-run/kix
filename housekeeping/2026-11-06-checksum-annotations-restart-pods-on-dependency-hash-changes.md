# 2026-11-06 · `kix/lib` pod template checksums · A change to nothing but an annotation on a network policy restarts grafana, loki-logs, and immich-server pods.

A pod template's `checksum/<name>` annotation is the identity hash of a ConfigMap or Secret, and that hash covers the object's `kix.run/depends-on` annotation. When a dependency of the ConfigMap changes its hash for an annotation-only reason (the 0.6.0 `kix.run/holds-data` stamp, the `failed` list added to `kix.run/readiness` on Cilium policies), the ConfigMap's hash moves, the checksum moves, and the pods restart although their configuration is the same. Seen by comparing `nix build ./ludo#cluster-ludo-kind` before and after the Cilium change: 146 of 149 changed objects differ only in annotations, and the three pod templates that changed all changed through such a checksum. Each release that adds an annotation needs an upgrade note listing the affected workloads, and users get restarts that carry no information.
