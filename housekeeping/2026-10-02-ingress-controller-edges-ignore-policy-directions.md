# 2026-10-02 · `kix/lib/vocab/compose.nix`, `kix/packages/tailscale` · The ingress-controller edge of `kix.expose` and tailscale's `out.mkBackendPolicy` ignore `networkPolicy.directions`.

Both build an ingress-only policy whenever the scope has an enforcer. Under `networkPolicy.directions = "egress"` default deny leaves ingress open, so that policy is the first ingress policy on the backend pods and closes them to every client except the controller or the tailscale proxies. `kix.operandPolicy` drops such sections by reading `scope.networkPolicyDirections`; `controllerEdgeFor` in `compose.nix` and `backendPolicy` in `kix/packages/tailscale/default.nix` do not read it.
