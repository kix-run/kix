# 2026-11-24 · `kix/lib/eval/import.nix` `mkImport` · An import cannot say that the environment reads an annotation domain, so platform annotations always warn.

`architecture.annotationDomainOwnership` accepts a domain that a package declares in `meta.annotationDomains`, but `kix.mkImport` builds its `meta` from `roles` and `scope` only. A flavor that knows its platform reads `eks.amazonaws.com` or `azure.workload.identity` annotations has no way to declare that, and no flavor adds the domain to `scorecard.knownAnnotationDomains` either. Every cluster on EKS, GKE, or AKS that sets a cloud identity annotation gets the warning, and the external-dns guide tells the reader to silence it by hand. See `v2_discussions/177_workload_cloud_identity.md`.
