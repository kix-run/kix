# 2026-10-04 · `kix/packages/clickhouse-keeper` · clickhouse-keeper is not scraped, so a Keeper quorum that loses its leader raises no alert.

The operator's default Keeper config (`kix/packages/clickhouse-operator/files/chk/keeper_config.d/01-keeper-01-default-config.xml`) has no `<prometheus>` section, the package declares no monitor, and the operator's exporter does not read Keeper. A lost quorum shows only as ClickHouseKeeperSessionLost and ClickHouseReplicaReadOnly on the servers. Scraping it needs the `prometheus/*` settings on the CHK and a PodMonitor with `portNumber` (a serviceTemplate would replace the operator's `zk` and `raft` ports). Rules worth writing then: no leader (`ClickHouseAsyncMetrics_KeeperIsLeader`, checking what a one-node quorum reports), synced followers below `replicas - 1`, and `KeeperIsExceedingMemorySoftLimitHit`.
