# 2026-09-20 · `kix/packages/promtail` · On a redeploy of ludo-kind-gateway, promtail's DaemonSet stayed 0/1 ready and the deploy failed on it.

What happened, in order, on a fresh single-node kind cluster (`kix-ludo-gw`, Kubernetes v1.36.1, Cilium from the `cilium` package with `policyEnforcementMode = always`), deploying `ludo-kind-gateway` from `ludo/` with a CLI built from PR #125:

1. First `kix deploy` (20:16 to 20:20). promtail's DaemonSet was created and reported ready (`DaemonSet/promtail@monitoring-system> ready`) after `Deployment/loki-gateway@monitoring-loki> ready`. The deploy ended with one unrelated failure (the EnvoyProxy CRD was missing, see the kind gateway demo entry), so the Activation was cancelled.
2. Second `kix deploy` at 20:24:06, same manifests. Every node was `configured`; `Deployment/loki-gateway@monitoring-loki` was configured and ready again. `DaemonSet/promtail@monitoring-system` was configured and then failed at about 20:27:30 with `no progress for 180s (idle budget 180s): DaemonSet/promtail: 0/1 ready`. Its Service, PackageInstance, ServiceMonitor and the Activation were cancelled as dependents. The deploy summary was `6 created, 258 configured, 0 unchanged, 1 failed, 4 cancelled`.
3. The promtail pod (`promtail-d7v5j`) was the pod from the first deploy: 8m36s old at 20:27, `Running`, 0 restarts, container `ready=false`. The DaemonSet's only event was `SuccessfulCreate` from the first deploy, so nothing rolled the pod.
4. The readiness probe is `GET /ready` on port `http-metrics`, initial delay 10s, period 10s, failure threshold 5, timeout 1s.
5. The last promtail log line, at 20:24:14 (eight seconds into the second deploy), was `error sending batch, will retry` with `status=-1` against `loki-gateway.monitoring-loki.svc.cluster.local`. `status=-1` is a transport failure, not an HTTP status. The pod's earlier lines were routine tailer messages for the envoy-gateway certgen pod logs.

Rendered policies around this path, all `Valid=True` at the time: `monitoring-system/promtail-daemonset-promtail-egress`, `monitoring-system/promtail-daemonset-promtail-apiserver-egress`, `monitoring-loki/promtail-daemonset-promtail-to-loki-gateway-ingress`, and the loki-gateway egress and ingress policies.

Not established: why the push to loki-gateway failed during the redeploy when it had worked minutes earlier, and whether promtail's `/ready` depends on a successful push. The cluster was deleted before that was checked. Two candidate causes worth ruling out when it recurs: the redeploy re-applies the Cilium policies and loki-gateway Deployment and briefly breaks the path, and promtail never returns to ready once its client backs off. The readiness declarations in PR #125 do not touch DaemonSets, and the same DaemonSet was ready on the first deploy of the same build.
