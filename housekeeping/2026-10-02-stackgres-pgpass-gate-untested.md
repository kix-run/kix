# 2026-10-02 · `kix/packages/stackgres-cluster` · Replicas may lose streaming after a restart on storage other than local-path, and nobody has checked.

StackGres issue #1441 (open on 2026-10-01) reports Patroni's pgpass file going back to mode 0660 when a pod starts, after which libpq refuses it and a replica cannot stream from the primary. `stackgres-pgpass-perms-fix` worked around it with a cluster-wide `pods/exec` grant and is deleted. The default is now 2 instances at availability pod and node, and 3 at zone, which is the case the workaround served.

On kind with local-path volumes the problem does not appear (W6 run K2b, 2026-10-02): with 2 instances, after the replica pod was deleted, `stat -c %a /var/lib/postgresql/pgpass` in its patroni container printed 600 and `patronictl list` showed the replica `streaming`. The suspected cause, the kubelet's recursive fsGroup chmod at mount, depends on the volume plugin, so a ZFS or other CSI volume can still behave differently. The same check on such a cluster settles it; the candidate fix if the mode reverts is `pods.persistentVolume.fsGroupChangePolicy = "OnRootMismatch"` on the SGCluster.
