`kix/package-eval.nix` builds every package with `clusterDomain = "eval.kix.test"`, and the `architecture.clusterDomainLiteral` rule finds the literal in openebs (`openebs-etcd`), valkey (`valkey-scripts`, also reached through harbor, immich and n8n), loki (its chart ConfigMaps, also reached through promtail) and redis. Each is listed in `knownFailures` through `domainLiteral`, `domainLiteralViaValkey` or `domainLiteralViaLoki`. openebs and valkey also write the literal in source and sit in `sourceDomainLiteralExceptions`. Remove a package's entries when it derives the domain from `out.fqdn` or `clusterConfig.clusterDomain`, and delete this file when no entry is left.
