# 2026-10-01 · `kix/packages/cilium-external/netpol-rules.nix` · The DNS egress rule every workload gets names `kube-system` and `k8s-app: kube-dns` instead of reading the `dns` provider.

`dnsEgressRule` allows egress only to pods in `kube-system` labelled `k8s-app: kube-dns`. A managed DNS provider in another namespace, or with other labels, is unreachable under network policy, and nothing in the graph says so. The coredns package refuses to build outside `kube-system` when `networkPolicy.enable` is set, which covers coredns only. Deriving the namespace and selector from the `dns` provider's `out` would retire that check, and touches `plugins/netpol/derive.nix`, the cilium rule builder and every flavour's `platform-dns` import.
