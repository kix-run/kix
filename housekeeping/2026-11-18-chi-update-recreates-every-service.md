# 2026-11-18 · `kix/packages/experimental/clickhouse-cluster` · Each change to a ClickHouseInstallation makes the operator delete and recreate all of its Services.

After an image change, the ClickHouseInstallation `ch` in `clusters/test-clickhouse.nix` listed `Update Service: analytics-ha/chi-ch-default-0-0 failed with error: just recreate the service in case of service type change ''=>'ClusterIP'` in `.status.errors`, for every replica Service and for `clickhouse-ch`. The Services' creation times matched the rollout, so the operator recreated them. Seen with clickhouse-operator 0.27.4. A recreated Service drops connections and can get a new ClusterIP. The operator probably compares a service template with no `spec.type` against the live `ClusterIP`; check whether the package's service templates should set `type: ClusterIP`.
