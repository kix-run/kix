# 2026-10-01 · `kix/lib/flavors/scaleway.nix` · Kix network policy cannot use Kapsule's managed Cilium.

The scaleway flavour binds `cni` as `exclusive` (provider "cilium (kapsule-managed)"), so a `cilium` instance is refused and `networkPolicy.enable` has no enforcer, although Kapsule runs Cilium. Binding it `shadowable` and declaring a `platform-cilium` import with `out.helmChartConfig = null` would let a `cilium` instance enforce Kix policy, but the flavour declares no platform intents for Kapsule's kube-system pods, so kube-system's default-deny would cut them off. It needs a Kapsule cluster to confirm the pods' labels and ports first.
