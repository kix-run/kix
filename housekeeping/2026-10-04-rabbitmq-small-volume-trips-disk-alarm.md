# 2026-10-04 · `kix/packages/rabbitmq-cluster` · A volume of 2 GB or less starts RabbitMQ in a disk alarm.

The RabbitMQ Cluster Operator writes `disk_free_limit.absolute = 2GB` into every node's rabbitmq.conf (internal/resource/configmap.go at v2.23.0). `storage.size` accepts any quantity, so a volume of 2Gi or less, whose free space RabbitMQ reads from the mounted filesystem, holds the free disk alarm from the first boot, and the cluster blocks every publisher. Evaluation does not refuse it. clusters/test-rabbitmq.nix uses 1Gi volumes and avoids the alarm on kind only because the local-path volumes report the host disk's free space.
