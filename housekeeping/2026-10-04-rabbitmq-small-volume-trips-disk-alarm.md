# 2026-10-04 · `kix/packages/rabbitmq-cluster` · A volume of 2 GB or less starts RabbitMQ in a disk alarm.

The RabbitMQ Cluster Operator writes `disk_free_limit.absolute = 2GB` into every node's rabbitmq.conf (internal/resource/configmap.go at v2.23.0). `storage.size` accepts any quantity, so a volume of 2Gi or less, whose free space RabbitMQ reads from the mounted filesystem, holds the free disk alarm from the first boot, and the cluster blocks every publisher. Evaluation warns below 5GB but does not refuse it, and the package does not raise the limit's headroom or lower the limit for small volumes. On kind the local-path volumes report the host disk's free space, so the alarm does not show there whatever `storage.size` says.
