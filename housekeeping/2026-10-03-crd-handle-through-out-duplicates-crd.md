# 2026-10-03 · `kix/lib` (CRD handles in `out`) · A CRD handle passed on through a second package's `out` makes the reader render the CustomResourceDefinition again.

When `cnpg-cluster` republished its operator's `out.crds` and `cnpg-database` built a Database from `postgres.out.crds.Database`, evaluation failed with "cluster-scoped CustomResourceDefinition databases.postgresql.cnpg.io ... is rendered by 2 instances: cnpg-system/cnpg-operator, data/app-db". A handle read straight from the operator dependency does not do this. So a package that builds an operator's custom resources must take the operator as a dependency of its own, even when another dependency could hand it the handles. Nothing documents this, and the error names the duplicate CRD rather than the handle that carried it.
