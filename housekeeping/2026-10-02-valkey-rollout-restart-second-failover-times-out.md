# 2026-10-02 · `kix/packages/valkey` · A rollout restart of a Sentinel valkey can leave the last primary's preStop failover waiting out its 25 s.

In the W6 kind run K2b, `kubectl rollout restart statefulset/valkey` restarted valkey-2 (the primary) first, and its preStop failed over to valkey-0. About 6 s later valkey-0's own preStop asked Sentinel for a second failover, logged "prestop: no other pod became the primary in time" after 25 s, and the pod stopped with writes paused for most of that time. Sentinel promoted valkey-2 afterwards and no data was lost. Sentinel delays a new failover of the same primary for up to twice `failover-timeout` (10 s here), and the StatefulSet's reverse-ordinal restart puts two failovers that close together whenever the primary is not the highest ordinal. Scripts: `kix/packages/valkey/scripts/prestop.sh`.
