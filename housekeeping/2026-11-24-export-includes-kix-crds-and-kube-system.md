# 2026-11-24 · `cli/kix-cli/src/commands/export.rs` · `kix export` includes Kix's CRDs and the `kube-system` Namespace in plain YAML meant for another tool.

By default, `kix export` on a `kind`-flavored cluster writes `_cluster/CustomResourceDefinition-activations.kix.run.yaml`, `_cluster/CustomResourceDefinition-packageinstances.kix.run.yaml`, and `_cluster/Namespace-kube-system.yaml`. When Flux applies that export and its `Kustomization` is later removed with pruning enabled, Flux deletes both Kix CRDs and wipes every `Activation` and `PackageInstance` record on the cluster. Flux also tries to delete `kube-system`, which the API server refuses, leaving the `Kustomization` stuck until suspended. `export-plain-yaml.mdx` mentions the CRDs, but omits `Namespace-kube-system.yaml` and what pruning does to a shared cluster.
