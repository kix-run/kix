# 2026-10-01 · `kix/packages/{victoria-metrics-agent,victoria-metrics-alert,victoria-metrics-cluster,clickhouse-keeper}` · The VMAgent, VMAlert, VMCluster, and ClickHouse Keeper pod specs carry a fixed `podAntiAffinity` rule.

BEST-PRACTICES §6.1 says packages do not write spread or anti-affinity: Kix derives spread from the availability level, and for pods an operator creates, `kix.availability.spreadConstraints { level; selector; topology; }` produces the rule. These rules use a fixed weight and fixed topology keys (the cluster's failure-domain keys in the VictoriaMetrics packages, `kubernetes.io/hostname` in clickhouse-keeper) at every level, so they do not follow the instance's availability. The rule is the `antiAffinity` helper in the victoria-metrics-cluster and victoria-metrics-alert packages, an inline `podAntiAffinity` in victoria-metrics-agent, and the `affinity` block of the keeper pod template in clickhouse-keeper.
