# 2026-11-18 · `kix/packages/experimental/clickhouse-operator` · Each Kix deploy that rewrites the bookkeeping annotations on a ClickHouseKeeperInstallation restarts every Keeper pod.

The Altinity operator copies a CR's `metadata.annotations` into the pod template of every StatefulSet it makes, and it includes those annotations in the hash it compares (`clickhouse-keeper.altinity.com/object-version`). `kix deploy` writes `kix.run/activations` and `kix.run/applied-hash` on the CHK whenever the activation changes, even when the plan says "tracking annotation only; no spec change or restart". The operator then rolls all Keeper replicas one at a time. Seen on kind with `clusters/test-clickhouse-operator-upgrade.nix` and operator 0.27.3, the version on main: `kubectl annotate chk keeper kix.run/activations=annotation-probe --overwrite` replaced all three Keeper pods, and the same edit on the ClickHouseInstallation restarted nothing. The quorum held each time, but a cluster loses a Keeper replica for each step of the roll on every deploy, whatever the deploy changes.

Options: set the operator's `annotation.exclude` in `builtinOperatorConfig` (`operator.nix`) to the `kix.run/*` keys Kix rewrites on each deploy (matched exactly, no wildcards), which restarts the operand pods once more; or have Kix keep its per-deploy bookkeeping out of `metadata.annotations` on operator CRs. Other operators that copy CR annotations into pods have the same problem.
