# 2026-10-04 · `kix/packages/clickhouse-operator` · The operator's metrics exporter series carry the operator's namespace, not the installation's.

The `ch-metrics` endpoint (port 8888) of the operator's ServiceMonitor has no `honorLabels`, so Prometheus renames the exporter's own `namespace` label to `exported_namespace` and sets `namespace` to the operator's. Every `chi_clickhouse_*` series then routes and groups under the operator's namespace, which is why the ClickHouse alerts read the built-in endpoint through clickhouse-cluster's PodMonitor instead. The exporter also copies every label and annotation of the ClickHouseInstallation onto every series (`pkg/metrics/operator/labels.go` at release-0.27.3), Kix's `kix.run/depends-on` among them, unless `metrics.labels.exclude` drops them. With clickhouse-cluster scraping each replica itself, each server is now read twice; the exporter could be turned off or its series dropped. `kix.monitor` cannot set `honorLabels` (see 2026-10-02-kix-monitor-has-no-honor-labels.md).
