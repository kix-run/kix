# 2026-10-03 · `kix/packages/external-secrets` · SecretStore and ClusterSecretStore declare no fact behaviour, so a store on an in-cluster provider gets no network policy.

A store whose `server` is a Kix Service address, such as `openbao.out.externalSecretsProvider`, makes every build trace "references a Service address but does not declare how its controller uses it" for the ClusterSecretStore and the ExternalSecret, and Kix assumes `conduct`. No policy then lets the external-secrets controller reach the provider, so under default deny the store cannot sync until the cluster adds policies of its own (`clusters/test-openbao.nix`, HANDBOOK §21.10). The CRD handles could declare `factBehavior.endpoint = "terminate"` with the controller's pods as `podSelector`, but see `2026-10-03-cluster-scoped-cr-operand-policy-in-wrong-namespace.md` for ClusterSecretStore.
