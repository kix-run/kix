---

Activation retention is applied by `kix gc`. Choose a minimum history, a
maximum history, and an age limit each time you run or schedule garbage
collection.

## Use the default policy

The default invocation applies this policy to Superseded Activations:

- always keep the 10 newest records;
- never keep more than 50 records;
- beyond the newest 10, retire records older than 30 days.

Run garbage collection without retention flags to use those defaults:

<Command commands={["kix gc production"]} cwd="kix-project/" />

Kix prints the records it plans to retire and asks for confirmation before it
deletes them.

## Set a retention policy

Use `--keep`, `--keep-max`, and `--keep-for` together when your policy differs
from the defaults. This example always keeps the newest 20 records, retains no
more than 100, and retires older records after 90 days once they are outside
the protected newest 20:

<Command
  commands={[
    "kix gc production --keep 20 --keep-max 100 --keep-for 90d",
  ]}
  cwd="kix-project/"
/>

The minimum wins if `--keep-max` is lower than `--keep`.

`--keep-for` accepts days, hours, minutes, or seconds, such as `30d`, `12h`,
`45m`, or `30s`. A bare number means days.

## Keep records by count only

Disable the age rule with `--keep-for off` or `--keep-for 0`:

<Command
  commands={[
    "kix gc production --keep 10 --keep-max 50 --keep-for off",
  ]}
  cwd="kix-project/"
/>

Kix then keeps between 10 and 50 of the newest Superseded records, regardless
of age.

## Schedule the same policy

Pass the complete policy in automation rather than relying on an operator's
shell defaults:

<Command
  commands={[
    "kix gc production --keep 20 --keep-max 100 --keep-for 90d --yes",
  ]}
  cwd="kix-project/"
/>

`--yes` skips the destructive confirmation, so use it only after reviewing the
same policy and target cluster interactively.

Retired Activations no longer appear as rollback targets. The next deployment
removes their hashes from the activation history stored on managed resources.

:::note[Use it in a task]
See [Garbage-collect stale resources](/docs/how-to/operate-a-cluster/garbage-collect-stale-resources/)
to choose between normal, hard-scan, and operator garbage collection.
:::
